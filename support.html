Frequently Asked Questions

  • Wait times look stale or wrong. Pull down on the park screen to refresh, and confirm you have a network connection. Live data refreshes automatically every 2 minutes.
  • I'm not getting wait-time or geofence alerts. Check Settings → Notifications on your device and confirm Location permission is set to "Always" for background geofence alerts.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Manage or cancel any subscription directly in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions — MagiCalPulse does not process payments directly.
  • Lightning Lane return times aren't showing for a ride. Not every attraction offers Lightning Lane, and return windows appear only while Disneyland is publishing one. A window dated tomorrow is shown as such rather than as available now.
